Suzume is the final installment of Makoto Shinkai's "Disaster trilogy" and this one was my favourite by far.

君の名は and 天気の子 focused quite heavily on the romantic relationship built in their respective movies, whereas Suzume mainly highlighted Suzume's relationship with her aunt and how she dealt with grief. As the movie progressed, we are introduced to numerous characters who showed kindness to Suzume which is contrasted with the exposition on how she lost her mother later on in the movie.

We are first introduced to the concept of ミミズ (worms) which are entities living in the "Ever-after". When they cross into the world of the living through gates, they cause earthquakes to occur. In order to prevent this from occuring, individuals dubbed "closers" are responsible for closing these gates whenever necessary.
